FAQ

Good to know.

A few quick answers before you get started.

Is CONTENT OS a separate app?

No. CONTENT OS is a workspace template built in Notion. You use it inside your own Notion account.

Do I need to know Notion?

Basic familiarity with Notion will help. You can also ask us for guidance on setup and getting started.

Can I customize it?

Yes. You can adapt database entries, properties and views to match your content workflow.

How do I get access?
